A free, open-source player for Amiga, tracker and Commodore 64 music (MOD, XM, S3M, IT, AHX, SID and many more). It searches the Modland archive and Amiga Music Preservation (AMP), downloads what you pick and plays it with libopenmpt, the HivelyTracker replayer, UADE, an emulated Amiga that runs the original replay routines of exotic formats such as PreTracker, or libsidplayfp, an emulated C64, for the High Voltage SID Collection.

• Player with one oscilloscope and level meter per channel, the running pattern and the texts inside the module
• Tap a channel to mute it, hold to hear it alone (tracker formats)
• Tracker views in the style of ProTracker, FastTracker 2, Impulse Tracker, Scream Tracker 3, HivelyTracker and OctaMED, and player windows for the Amiga formats and the C64
• Files with several songs: choose the song, or let them play one after another
• Format filter: show only the kinds of music you want
• Shuffle and repeat
• Background playback with lock-screen and headset controls
• Library with favourites, playlists, search and sorting; import your own files
• Radio: random modules from Modland or AMP, modules that sound alike, or your favourites
• Material 3 with dynamic colour and several colour designs

Modelled on the iOS app 4champ by Aleksi Sitomaniemi. No ads, no tracking. Developed with the help of an AI coding assistant. An independent project, not affiliated with Amiga Music Preservation (AMP) or Modland.

This build contains only the UADE replay routines that have a free licence. A "full" build with about a hundred more Amiga formats is available from the project's GitHub page.
